# Privacy Statement

Currency Converter does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal
information.

The extension stores your last selected amount, source currency, and target
currency locally on your device using Chrome's `storage.local` API. This data is
used only to restore your previous converter settings when you reopen the
popup.

When you use the converter, the extension sends the selected currencies and
amount directly to the Frankfurter exchange-rate API to calculate the result.
Frankfurter provides European Central Bank reference rates and does not require
an account or API key from this extension.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, identity services,
browsing history access, page content access, or background tracking.

For questions, open an issue at:
https://github.com/PotterA011/currency-converter-extension/issues
